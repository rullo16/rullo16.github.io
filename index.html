Featured research

Teaching a drone swarm to fly through clutter

For my MSc dissertation, carried out with Kyushu University, I trained drones in a Unity simulation to reach their goals using only their own camera and sensors. On open levels the MAPPO policy matches a hand-tuned controller that is handed the exact goal direction. On the cluttered level it is the only method that succeeds: 99.5% against 67%.

The ablations show what mattered most. Without the staged curriculum, success on that level drops from 99.5% to 8.5%.
